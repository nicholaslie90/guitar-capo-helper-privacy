Guitar Capo Helper

Privacy Policy · Effective 12 October 2026

In short: the app itself collects nothing about you and has no accounts or servers. Your songs stay on your phone. Microphone audio is analysed on the phone and never recorded or sent. If ads are shown, Google's ad SDK collects some data as described below.

Who we are

Guitar Capo Helper (package dev.nicholaslie.capohelper) is developed by Nicholas Lie. Contact: nicholaslie90@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

  • Settings and selections (keys, capo positions, preferences) and songs and setlists you create are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. You can export or import a backup file yourself; where that file goes is your choice.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information.

Microphone

The optional Listen for the key feature uses the microphone only while that screen is open. Audio is analysed in memory on your device to estimate the musical key; it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you buy Remove ads, the app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic information, for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform), and you can change your choice later under Settings → Privacy options.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase belongs to your Google account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
